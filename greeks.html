The Four Forces

Delta
Direction. How much the option price moves per $1 move in the stock. 0.50 = 50¢ per dollar. At 1.00 you're moving dollar-for-dollar with the stock.
0.50
Γ
Gamma
Acceleration. The rate at which Delta changes. High Gamma = Delta is unstable. Near-the-money options explode with Gamma. This is the force amateur calculators miss.

Theta
Decay. What the contract costs you every day you hold it. The clock is always running. Theta accelerates exponentially in the final 30 days.

Volatility. Gain or loss per 1% change in Implied Volatility. After earnings, IV crushes and Vega becomes destructive — even if you called the direction right.
