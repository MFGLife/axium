      What is Axuim.Church?

      A browser-native narrative forensics engine that analyzes text structure, semantic coherence, emotional manipulation patterns, and rhetorical integrity — without any server-side processing.

      How it works

      Pure heuristic analysis using lexical statistics, pattern matching, Flesch readability formulas, n-gram repetition, tense analysis, and 20+ weighted signals to produce a Narrative Integrity Index.

      Privacy

      All analysis runs locally in your browser. No text is ever sent to a server. No data is stored beyond your session history (localStorage only).

      Use Cases

      Academic writing review, journalism fact-density check, propaganda detection, legal document analysis, speech writing, creative fiction coherence testing.

      Scoring Model

      20 metrics, each weighted by linguistic significance. Scores range 0–100. The final Integrity Index is a weighted composite. Sensitivity and domain modes shift the weighting profiles.

      Limitations

      Heuristic analysis cannot replace human judgment. Scores reflect structural patterns only — not truth, accuracy, or intent. Always use contextually.
