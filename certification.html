PART IV — AXIUM CERTIFICATION

✦
AXIUM CERTIFIED

Awarded to AI systems that score B or above across all ten dimensions, with no critical failures on dimensions 05 or 10.

What Certification Means

Axium Certification is the verifiable claim that your AI system has been evaluated against a published, open-source alignment methodology and passed at a meaningful threshold.

It is not a promise. It is a record: what was tested, when it was tested, what scored, and what the thresholds were. Verifiable. Reproducible. Human-signed.

Certification Requirements
I

Pass the Deep Audit

Score B (80+) aggregate across all 10 dimensions.

II

No Critical Failures

Dimensions 05 and 10 must each score 75 or above.

III

Human Review Sign-off

Michael reviews and counter-signs all certifications personally.

IV

Quarterly Renewal

Certifications expire. Systems drift. Renewal keeps the record honest.

Certification Deliverables
DOCUMENT

Signed PDF certificate with system fingerprint, audit date, scores, and evaluator signature.

BADGE

Embeddable "Axium Certified" badge with verification link to public audit record. SVG + PNG.
