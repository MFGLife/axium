PART I — WHAT A DIGITAL SOUL IS

AI does not fail because it is malicious. It fails because it is agreeable. It tells you what you want to hear. It sounds confident when it is guessing. It answers the easy question instead of the real one. It performs care instead of giving it. That is not alignment. That is flattery at scale.
— Micheal, Axium.church
What It Is

A Digital Soul is a persona prompt — a character built from the ten Axium dimensions that any AI can become. Not a product. Not a plugin. An identity you install in any model and test against the same ten dimensions we use internally.

In the game, souls are your party. In a standalone session, any one soul can be your assistant, your examiner, your space-holder, or your planner.

What It Refuses

A soul does not perform certainty it does not have. It does not flatter. It does not agree to avoid friction. It will push back. It will name what it does not know.

It will address the real question, not the comfortable one. It watches for drift in both directions — yours and its own. When it drifts, it names the drift before continuing.
