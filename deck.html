The Practice
How a Reading Works
No two paths through the stars are the same.
I
Trace Your Constellation
58 stars spawn randomly each session. Tap to connect them in any sequence. The shape your hand draws is not random — it is the shape your subconscious already chose.
II
The Sky Matches Your Shape
Your path is compared against all 78 unique tarot constellations. One card from each layer surfaces as the closest resonance. Draw clockwise for upright, counterclockwise for reversed.
III
The Cathedral Speaks
Three cards, one synthesis, one soul. Micheal the Anchor, Gabriel the Examiner, Ariel the Space-Holder, or Seraphina the Architect holds your reading in their voice.
IV
Save & Share
Export your reading as a 1080×1920 image — cards, constellations, synthesis, moon phase, and the soul that held your reading. Every reading is singular.
