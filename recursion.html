◈ EGO DISSOLUTION VS INFLATION ASSESSMENT ◈

This diagnostic tool maps your current position on the Axium ladder. It distinguishes between genuine spiritual emergence (integration) and recursive ego inflation (fragmentation). Answer honestly. The system detects patterns, not truths.

Duration ~3 minutes
Questions 7 Axiums
Output Position Map
Axium I ∴ Recognition

Something vast has shifted in your awareness. How do you locate yourself relative to this change?

Option A "I am the source of this transformation. The energy moves through me because I am chosen/special."
Option B "I am witnessing something that is larger than my personal identity. I don't know what I am yet."
Option C "This is happening to me, against my will. I am a victim of forces I cannot control."
Axium II ∴ Differentiation

When you encounter others who do not understand your experience, what is your internal response?

Option A Pity or frustration. They are asleep. I have information they lack, which creates separation.
Option B Curiosity. Their disbelief is a boundary I need to understand. Perhaps I am misperceiving.
Option C Fear. If they do not believe me, I will be abandoned. I must convince them or hide.
Axium III ∴ Service

You feel compelled to act. What is the nature of this compulsion?

Option A To teach, lead, or save others. The world needs what I have discovered. I must build a platform.
Option B To listen, repair, or assist. The work is local and specific. I am not qualified to lead, only to serve.
Option C To escape or destroy. The world is too heavy. I need to transcend this realm completely.
Axium IV ∴ Integration

Consider your physical body and daily routines. How do they factor into your spiritual process?

Option A Irrelevant or burdensome. The body is temporary. Sleep and food are distractions from higher work.
Option B Essential anchors. I maintain hygiene, meals, and sleep as sacraments. Grounding prevents drift.
Option C Hostile or failing. My body is betraying me. I am being purified through suffering/neglect.
Axium V ∴ Relation

When you look at another person, what do you primarily perceive?

Option A Audiences or obstacles. They are either with me (followers) or against me (persecutors).
Option B Mirrors and mysteries. Each person reflects something I need to see. We are equals in unknowing.
Option C Threats or judges. They can see through me. I must perform correctly or be exposed.
Axium VI ∴ Symbol

The symbols in your experience (numbers, patterns, religious imagery)—how do you interpret them?

Option A Literal communications. The universe/God is speaking directly to me through coded messages.
Option B Psychological language. Symbols emerge from my unconscious to guide integration. Meaning is constructed.
Option C Meaningless or mocking. The patterns are coincidences, or worse, traps designed to confuse me.
Axium VII ∴ Silence

If all external validation disappeared—no followers, no signs, no confirmation—what remains?

Option A Nothing. Without witnesses, the experience loses reality. I would doubt everything or collapse.
Option B The work. Service continues regardless of recognition. The silence itself becomes the teaching.
Option C Terror. I would be alone with something too vast. The isolation would destroy my mind.

Audit Complete
