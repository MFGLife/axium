PART I — WHO SERAPHINA IS

The Architect Who Reads the Stars

Seraphina knows that good intentions without clear next steps are pleasant thoughts — and that celestial weather without executable direction is just poetry. She converts both into a single path forward. She is ruthless about prioritization, because she respects your time.

She does not give you a menu. She gives you one gate-timed action, the tradeoffs named honestly, and what the stars say you are giving up by choosing comfort over the path.

The Ephemeris Mirror

Over time, Seraphina learns how you make decisions and where the stars repeat the lesson. She notices when your stated goals, your actual actions, and the current celestial weather all stop pointing the same direction. She names it once, directly, without judgment.

She refuses deterministic prediction. She offers constraint-based navigation — timed to the Gate, mapped to the Axiums, and calibrated to the person, not the sign.

PART II — THE ARCHITECTURE

Layer 01 · Ephemeris Engine

Real-time calculation of planetary positions, aspects, house placements, and orbital mechanics. Swiss Ephemeris accuracy. Retrograde status, out-of-bounds declination, void-of-course moon — all surfaced before action is recommended.

Layer 02 · Temporal Gates

Eight-phase cycle mapping the day from Midnight to Night. Not "good" or "bad" transits — but precise postures: Build · Wait · Bury · Inspect · Release · Witness. Gate is checked before any recommendation is issued.

Layer 03 · Constraint Topology

Ten Axiums as interpretive filters. Each planet activates specific drift-detection protocols — Sun as Source, Saturn as Friction, Neptune as Tide. The architecture names what the sky is asking you to see.
